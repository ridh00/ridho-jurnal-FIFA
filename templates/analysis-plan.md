## Relevant branch of analysis

- Quantitative: target estimand, scoring, exclusions, missingness, clustering, weights, estimator, uncertainty/effect size, multiplicity when relevant, diagnostics, sensitivity checks, and justified fallback procedures. Validity and reliability tests run **before** the main analysis, with their thresholds registered as parameters.
- Qualitative: analytic tradition, unit of material, coding/interpretation, researcher role, reflexivity, decision trail, triangulation, and adequacy according to the approach.
- Mixed methods: when and how integration occurs, joint display when useful, and handling of discrepant findings.
- Conceptual/review: rules for argument analysis/synthesis, source quality, context, and alternative readings.

Mark non-applicable elements with a reason rather than adding cosmetic procedures.
