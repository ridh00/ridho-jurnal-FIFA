## Reverse outline (before submission)

Read only the first sentence of every paragraph. Does the argument read from those sentences alone? PASS / FAIL, with the paragraphs that need work: NOT ASSESSED.

## Claim-verb audit (FG4)

Causal verbs only where the design permits; *suggests* / *indicates* for associations and interpretations; consistent tenses. The title and the abstract are audited with the body — they are where hedges are most often dropped. Paragraphs checked and corrections made: NOT ASSESSED.
