Priority:

- **critical** — integrity, permission, identification, validity, or inference may fail;
- **major** — material argument, method, evidence, or consistency issue;
- **editorial** — does not change the substantive conclusion.

Do not use an aggregate score that allows many minor items to hide a critical failure.
