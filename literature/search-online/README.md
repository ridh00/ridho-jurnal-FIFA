# Search-online track — locus evidence (Stage 1)

Evidence that the phenomenon exists at the locus: official statistics, government and agency reports, organizational documents, reputable news, sector reports. One [source note](../../templates/source-note.md) per item, named by its `SRC` ID. Downloaded datasets also receive a `DAT` record. Nothing has been collected yet.

Search parameters come from the [locus-evidence preset](../../parameters/presets/search/locus-evidence.md); every run is logged in the search log with a `RUN` ID. This track establishes that something is happening; it does not establish a research gap and it does not enter the five-year literature matrix.
