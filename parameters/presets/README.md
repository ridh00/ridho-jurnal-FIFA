# Parameter presets

Defaults to copy into `parameters/` as `PRM` records. A preset is a starting point; every value in a copied record still needs a justification recorded for this project, and a changed value needs a reason.

| Preset | Type | Stage | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Locus evidence search](search/locus-evidence.md) | search | 1 | online evidence that the phenomenon exists at the locus |
| [Theory-development search](search/theory-development.md) | search | 2 | grand theory and its developments |
| [Empirical literature search](search/empirical-literature.md) | search | 3 | empirical studies of the phenomenon |
| [COREQ 2007](reporting/coreq-2007.md) | reporting | 5, 8 | interviews and focus groups |
| [SRQR 2014](reporting/srqr-2014.md) | reporting | 5, 8 | qualitative research, any design |
| [JARS-Qual 2018](reporting/jars-qual-2018.md) | reporting | 5, 8 | qualitative research, psychology and adjacent fields |
| [JARS-Quant 2018](reporting/jars-quant-2018.md) | reporting | 5, 8 | quantitative research, psychology and adjacent fields |
| [STROBE 2007](reporting/strobe-2007.md) | reporting | 5, 8 | observational and survey designs |
| [CONSORT 2010](reporting/consort-2010.md) | reporting | 5, 8 | randomized experiments |
| [TREND 2004](reporting/trend-2004.md) | reporting | 5, 8 | nonrandomized evaluations |
| [PLS-SEM 2019](reporting/pls-sem-2019.md) | reporting + analysis | 5, 6, 8 | partial least squares structural equation modeling |
| [GRAMMS 2008](reporting/gramms-2008.md) | reporting | 5, 8 | mixed methods |
| [JARS-Mixed 2018](reporting/jars-mixed-2018.md) | reporting | 5, 8 | mixed methods, psychology and adjacent fields |
| [PRISMA 2020](reporting/prisma-2020.md) | reporting | 3, 8 | systematic and structured reviews |
| [PRISMA 2020 for Abstracts](reporting/prisma-abstracts-2020.md) | reporting | 8 | the abstract of a systematic review |
| [CONSORT for Abstracts](reporting/consort-abstracts-2008.md) | reporting | 8 | the abstract of a randomized trial |

Item wording in the reporting presets is a short paraphrase for orientation. Verify every item against the official checklist before relying on it and record the version used. See [reporting standards](../../docs/reporting-standards.md) for the selection rule and caveats.
