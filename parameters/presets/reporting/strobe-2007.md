# Preset — STROBE (2007) reporting checklist

Type: `reporting`. Copy to `parameters/PRM-###-reporting-strobe.md` using the [reporting checklist](../../../templates/reporting-checklist.md) form. Applies to observational designs: cross-sectional surveys, cohort, and case-control studies. Several items have sub-items (a, b, c) in the official checklist; list the ones that apply.

Official source: von Elm, E., Altman, D. G., Egger, M., Pocock, S. J., Gøtzsche, P. C., & Vandenbroucke, J. P. (2007). The Strengthening the Reporting of Observational Studies in Epidemiology (STROBE) statement. *PLoS Medicine*, 4(10), e296. Item wording below is a short paraphrase; verify against the published checklist for the design (cross-sectional, cohort, case-control) and record the version used.

Status vocabulary: `ADDRESSED` (with location) / `NOT APPLICABLE` (with a design-based reason) / `OPEN`.
