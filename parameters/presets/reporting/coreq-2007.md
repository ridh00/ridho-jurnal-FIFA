# Preset — COREQ (2007) reporting checklist

Type: `reporting`. Copy to `parameters/PRM-###-reporting-coreq.md` using the [reporting checklist](../../../templates/reporting-checklist.md) form, then fill the manuscript location and status of every item. Applies to qualitative studies using interviews or focus groups.

Official source: Tong, A., Sainsbury, P., & Craig, J. (2007). Consolidated criteria for reporting qualitative research (COREQ): a 32-item checklist for interviews and focus groups. *International Journal for Quality in Health Care*, 19(6), 349–357. Item wording below is a short paraphrase; verify against the published checklist and record the version used.

Status vocabulary: `ADDRESSED` (with location) / `NOT APPLICABLE` (with a design-based reason) / `OPEN`.

Caveat: items 22 and 28 describe practices that are not coherent with every qualitative tradition. Under reflexive thematic analysis, mark them `NOT APPLICABLE` with the methodological reason rather than reporting a saturation or member check that was not conceptually meaningful.
