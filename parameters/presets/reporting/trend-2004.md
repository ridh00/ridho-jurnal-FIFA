# Preset — TREND (2004) reporting checklist

Type: `reporting`. Copy to `parameters/PRM-###-reporting-trend.md` using the [reporting checklist](../../../templates/reporting-checklist.md) form. Applies to nonrandomized evaluations of interventions (quasi-experiments).

Official source: Des Jarlais, D. C., Lyles, C., Crepaz, N., & the TREND Group. (2004). Improving the reporting quality of nonrandomized evaluations of behavioral and public health interventions: The TREND statement. *American Journal of Public Health*, 94(3), 361–366. The statement has 22 items; the section-level orientation below is a paraphrase. Verify every item and its sub-items against the published checklist before relying on it.

Status vocabulary: `ADDRESSED` (with location) / `NOT APPLICABLE` (with a design-based reason) / `OPEN`.
