# Preset — SRQR (2014) reporting checklist

Type: `reporting`. Copy to `parameters/PRM-###-reporting-srqr.md` using the [reporting checklist](../../../templates/reporting-checklist.md) form. Applies to qualitative research of any design.

Official source: O'Brien, B. C., Harris, I. B., Beckman, T. J., Reed, D. A., & Cook, D. A. (2014). Standards for reporting qualitative research: a synthesis of recommendations. *Academic Medicine*, 89(9), 1245–1251. Item wording below is a short paraphrase; verify against the published checklist and record the version used.

Status vocabulary: `ADDRESSED` (with location) / `NOT APPLICABLE` (with a design-based reason) / `OPEN`.
