# Preset — CONSORT (2010) reporting checklist

Type: `reporting`. Copy to `parameters/PRM-###-reporting-consort.md` using the [reporting checklist](../../../templates/reporting-checklist.md) form. Applies to randomized experiments. Most items have sub-items (a, b) in the official checklist; list the ones that apply. CONSORT was updated in 2025; record which version the target journal requires.

Official source: Schulz, K. F., Altman, D. G., & Moher, D. (2010). CONSORT 2010 statement: updated guidelines for reporting parallel group randomised trials. *BMJ*, 340, c332. Item wording below is a short paraphrase; verify against the published checklist and record the version used.

Status vocabulary: `ADDRESSED` (with location) / `NOT APPLICABLE` (with a design-based reason) / `OPEN`.
