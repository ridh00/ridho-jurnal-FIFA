# Preset — PRISMA 2020 reporting checklist

Type: `reporting`. Copy to `parameters/PRM-###-reporting-prisma.md` using the [reporting checklist](../../../templates/reporting-checklist.md) form. Applies to systematic and structured reviews presented as studies. Several items have sub-items (10a–b, 13a–f, 16a–b, 20a–d, 23a–d, 24a–c) in the official checklist; list the ones that apply. Use PRISMA-S for the search report and PRISMA-ScR for scoping reviews.

Official source: Page, M. J., McKenzie, J. E., Bossuyt, P. M., et al. (2021). The PRISMA 2020 statement: an updated guideline for reporting systematic reviews. *BMJ*, 372, n71. Item wording below is a short paraphrase; verify against the published checklist and record the version used.
