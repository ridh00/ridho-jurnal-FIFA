# Preset — APA JARS-Quant (2018) reporting standards

Type: `reporting`. Copy to `parameters/PRM-###-reporting-jars-quant.md` using the [reporting checklist](../../../templates/reporting-checklist.md) form. Applies to quantitative research in psychology and adjacent fields; journals outside psychology may prefer STROBE or CONSORT.

Official source: Appelbaum, M., Cooper, H., Kline, R. B., Mayo-Wilson, E., Nezu, A. M., & Rao, S. M. (2018). Journal article reporting standards for quantitative research in psychology: The APA Publications and Communications Board task force report. *American Psychologist*, 73(1), 3–25. The official tables have finer items and design-specific modules (experimental, nonexperimental, longitudinal, meta-analysis, replication). The section-level orientation below is a paraphrase; expand it from the official table for the design used.

Status vocabulary: `ADDRESSED` (with location) / `NOT APPLICABLE` (with a design-based reason) / `OPEN`.
