# Preset — APA JARS-Mixed (2018) reporting standards

Type: `reporting`. Copy to `parameters/PRM-###-reporting-jars-mixed.md` using the [reporting checklist](../../../templates/reporting-checklist.md) form. Applies to mixed-methods research in psychology and adjacent fields; register JARS-Quant or JARS-Qual (or STROBE, COREQ) for each strand as well.

Official source: Levitt, H. M., Bamberg, M., Creswell, J. W., Frost, D. M., Josselson, R., & Suárez-Orozco, C. (2018). Journal article reporting standards for qualitative primary, qualitative meta-analytic, and mixed methods research in psychology. *American Psychologist*, 73(1), 26–46. The official table has finer items; the section-level orientation below is a paraphrase. Expand it from the official table.

Status vocabulary: `ADDRESSED` (with location) / `NOT APPLICABLE` (with a design-based reason) / `OPEN`.
